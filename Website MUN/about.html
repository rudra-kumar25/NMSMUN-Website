History & Secretariat

Where NMSMUN came from, and the students responsible for running it this year.

The story so far

Four editions in.

NMSMUN began as a single-day, in-house simulation for senior students at GEMS New Millennium School and has grown into a multi-committee conference that invites delegations from across the region.

Each edition has added something: a second day, a crisis track, a formal press corps, and a marking rubric shared with delegates in advance. The through-line has stayed the same — substance over theatrics, and chairs who prepare as hard as the delegates.

The fourth edition continues that direction with fifteen committees across four tiers and the introduction of the school-wide Communities Cup.

Full dates and figures for past editions are being compiled and will be added here.
