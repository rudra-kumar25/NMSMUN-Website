From the Secretariat

A conference built on rigour, not spectacle.

Photo

Soham Janjirkar & Raphael Rusten

Secretaries-General, NMSMUN

Welcome to NMSMUN. This conference exists for delegates who want committee sessions that move, chairs who have read the same material you have, and debate that is judged on substance. That is the standard we hold ourselves to.

Every committee this year is built around a single question worth two days of argument.

You will be expected to arrive with a sourced position, to yield the floor gracefully, and to write resolutions that could survive contact with a real vote. In return, the Secretariat commits to fair marking, prompt feedback and a schedule that respects your time.

Whether this is your first placard or your fifteenth, there is a tier for you. Read the committee list, pick the room that will stretch you, and apply.

— Soham Janjirkar & Raphael Rusten, Secretaries-General
